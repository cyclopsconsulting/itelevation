Why this program matters

Move from change announcements to readiness, adoption, and sustained behavior.

Organizations often introduce new systems, processes, roles, and expectations without adequately preparing the people affected. Change Management connects leadership alignment, stakeholder engagement, communication, training, and adoption measurement to the transformation itself.

Pain point 01

Unclear case for change

People may not understand why the change is happening, what it means for them, or what success looks like.

Pain point 02

Low readiness

Stakeholders may be asked to adopt new tools or processes before they have the information, skills, or support they need.

Pain point 03

Resistance and disruption

Unaddressed concerns and disruption to established routines can slow adoption and reduce productivity.

Pain point 04

Benefits not sustained

Without reinforcement and feedback, people may revert to old behaviors and expected benefits may not materialize.

Program purpose

Help people understand, prepare for, and sustain change.

The Change Management program establishes the practices, roles, communication, training, readiness checks, and reinforcement routines needed to support adoption and sustain new ways of working.

Transformation journey

Eight steps from change impact assessment to sustained adoption.

The journey can be adapted to the transformation scope, stakeholder groups, readiness, and adoption risks.

Assess current state

Review transformation scope, stakeholder groups, current readiness, adoption risks, and change impacts.

Align leadership

Align leaders on the case for change, target behaviors, sponsorship needs, and stakeholder priorities.

Design the approach

Create the change strategy, communication plan, training approach, readiness model, and adoption metrics.

Activate engagement

Launch stakeholder engagement, sponsor routines, communication channels, and readiness activities.

Prioritize affected groups

Prioritize groups, locations, roles, and activities based on impact, readiness, risk, and business criticality.

Execute change activities

Deliver communications, training, coaching, readiness checks, resistance management, and transition support.

Measure adoption

Track readiness, adoption, sentiment, training completion, usage, behavior change, and business stabilization.

Reinforce and improve

Use feedback, lessons learned, adoption data, and reinforcement actions to sustain change.

Success measures

Measure readiness, adoption, and sustained behavior change.

Select measures that reflect the stakeholder groups, expected behaviors, and outcomes in scope.

Readiness

Stakeholder groups with completed impact assessments and readiness plans.

Adoption

Adoption indicators tied to expected behaviors and business outcomes.

Enablement

Leadership alignment, investment transparency, portfolio balance, capacity utilization, and confidence in prioritization decisions.

Start small, prove value

Start with a focused change readiness assessment.

A focused assessment identifies affected groups, readiness gaps, adoption risks, and the communication and training actions needed before scaling change activities.

  • Identify the most affected stakeholder groups and roles.
  • Assess change impacts, readiness, and sponsorship needs.
  • Define targeted messages, training, and engagement activities.
  • Establish readiness and adoption measures.
  • Pilot the approach, collect feedback, and refine before broader rollout.
Typical assessment outputs

A practical plan ready to activate

Change impact summary, stakeholder map, sponsorship plan, communications and training plan, readiness dashboard, adoption measures, and reinforcement recommendations.
