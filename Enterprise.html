Why this program matters

Move from project-by-project technology decisions to a coherent enterprise direction.

As organizations grow, technology decisions can create duplicated platforms, inconsistent standards, unclear dependencies, and limited visibility into the future state. Enterprise Architecture provides the structure to align technology with the business capabilities that matter most.

Pain point 01

Fragmented landscape

Applications, platforms, and integrations evolve independently, increasing complexity and limiting reuse.

Pain point 02

Unclear direction

Without a shared target state, teams make local decisions that may conflict with enterprise priorities.

Pain point 03

Duplicated investment

Overlapping capabilities and inconsistent standards can increase cost, risk, and delivery friction.

Pain point 04

Complex modernization

Legacy dependencies and unclear sequencing make it harder to modernize without disrupting operations.

Transformation journey

Eight steps from landscape assessment to continuous architecture improvement.

The journey can begin with a focused assessment and grow into an operating capability that guides modernization, simplification, and business alignment.

Assess current state

Review business priorities, capabilities, applications, data flows, integrations, platforms, and known pain points.

Map the landscape

Connect capabilities, systems, data, integrations, ownership, cost, risk, and business value.

Align stakeholders

Agree on architecture principles, target outcomes, decision rights, and modernization priorities.

Design target state

Define target architecture, reference models, standards, platform direction, and simplification opportunities.

Prioritize initiatives

Rank architecture gaps by business value, complexity reduction, risk, dependencies, and feasibility.

Build the roadmap

Sequence initiatives, dependencies, milestones, investment needs, and expected outcomes.

Activate governance

Launch architecture reviews, decision forums, exception management, and standards adoption across delivery teams.

Improve continuously

Refresh architecture views, standards, and roadmaps as business priorities and technology conditions evolve.

Success measures

Measure alignment, simplification, governance, and roadmap execution.

Select measures that reflect the organization’s capabilities, technology landscape, and transformation priorities.

Alignment

Business capabilities mapped to technology investments and architecture priorities.

Simplification

Redundant applications, platforms, and integrations identified for rationalization.

Governance & roadmap

Architecture decisions managed through clear standards and review cadence, with modernization initiatives sequenced against dependencies and outcomes.

Start small, prove value

Start with a focused architecture assessment.

A focused assessment reveals where complexity is slowing the organization down, which architecture decisions need governance, and where modernization can deliver the most business value.

  • Map a selected set of critical business capabilities and supporting systems.
  • Identify duplication, major dependencies, technical risks, and architecture gaps.
  • Agree on target-state principles and the most important decision guardrails.
  • Prioritize a small set of modernization and simplification opportunities.
  • Define a practical roadmap and governance cadence to guide next steps.
Typical assessment outputs

A clear direction for architecture change

Current-state findings, capability-to-system map, key architecture risks, target-state principles, prioritized opportunities, roadmap outline, and recommended governance routines.
